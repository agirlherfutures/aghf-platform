# A Girl & Her Futures™ · TikTok commercial (48s)

Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, silent with burned-in captions. Add the voiceover and music on top.

- Preview: open `commercial/tiktok.html` in a browser (space pauses, `?t=20` opens on one frame, `?guides=1` shows the TikTok safe zone).
- Render: `node commercial/render-tiktok.cjs` writes `out/aghf-tiktok-48s.mp4`. Use `--stills 5,20` to check single frames (add `--guides` for the safe zone).
- Scene code: `commercial/scenes-tiktok.js` (uses `../illustrations.js` for art primitives).

## Notes

- Safe zone: all text and key art stay out of the top 150 px, the bottom 400 px and the right 140 px.
- Compliance: no income or profit claims. The "guaranteed signal" alerts are crossed out and swiped away. "Trading involves risk." stays on the end card. Game points (GP) are in-app points, not money.
