## 1

Welcome to Lesson Eight: Right Analysis. Wrong Execution.

The read was right. The trade wasn’t.

## 2

Meet a navigator. She reads the chart perfectly: right island, right route.

Her plan: two crates, and wait for the green light.

Instead she loads six, and leaves on red, chasing the boats already out.

The green light comes after she’s gone.

On the way, she moves her safety line once.

And she still reaches the island.

The read was right. The trip wasn’t.

## 3

So how do we grade it? Not with one big smoothie.

Toss everything in the blender with the win, and it all tastes fine.

But the parts don’t blend away.

Analysis: good. Execution: a chase above the level. Risk: three times the planned size. Management: a moved stop.

Outcome: the target hit. Five separate grades.

Analysis good. Trade not good. The win doesn’t average them away.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

The read was right. The trade wasn’t. Being right about the market doesn’t erase being wrong about the execution.
