# Clean vs Messy Setups

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Five: Clean versus Messy Setups.

Valid isn’t the same as wanted.

## 2

Picture a beach after the tide. Every shell here is a real shell.

Shell A: clean, whole, plenty of room. Into the basket.

Shell B: valid, but a rock hangs right over it. Take it, or wait.

Shell C: cracked, sandy, tangled. Technically a shell. She passes.

Shell D is already floating off. Grabbing it means chasing. Pass.

Valid isn’t the same as wanted.

## 3

Now an antiques appraiser. Every piece gets three separate tags.

Validity, quality, decision. This vase: valid, A, take.

Next piece. Chipped, muddy, but genuine.

Valid. Quality: C. Decision: pass.

Valid, C, pass is a real combination.

A, B and C are review labels, not trading signals.

Validity is method compliance. Quality is the context review. Keep them apart.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

The goal isn’t to find ICC everywhere. The goal is to know which ICC you actually want.

## 5

Your mission.

Grade four setups with three separate labels, and ask: which ICC do I actually want?

Let’s find out.
