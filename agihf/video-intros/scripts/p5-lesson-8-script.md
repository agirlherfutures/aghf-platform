## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 12, Lesson 8 ("ICC + Market Structure"): two lenses on one chart: structure (H, HL, HH, HL, HH) asks what price is building; ICC (indication, correction, continuation) asks where price is in the directional sequence; catch the mistake "I learned ICC, so I don’t need market structure": without structure you can’t answer what level mattered, what actually indicated, whether the correction invalidated anything or whether continuation was meaningful; with both you get a read you can explain; "Structure asks what. ICC asks where in the sequence."
