# Why I Passed This Trade
*Passing can be the execution.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Six: Why I Passed This Trade.

Today, passing can be the execution.

## 2

Picture a lifeguard on her tower. The surf looks perfect.

The setup is valid, and the read is right.

Then the warning flag goes up: a rip current, right on schedule.

That’s your CPI release, three minutes out, and your rule says no entries five minutes either side.

So she stays on the tower. Passing isn’t failing to find a trade.

It’s executing the rule.

## 3

How do we grade that pass? Picture a bus stop.

The packed bus pulls up at the worst time, and she lets it go.

So where does that bus end up? Maybe it races to the top.

Maybe it turns right around, or just chops in traffic.

Every ending leaves a correct pass correct, because it’s graded on what was known at the stop.

And the next bus comes.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

Passing isn’t failing to find a trade. Passing can be the execution: the rule that says, this one isn’t mine.

## 5

Your mission.

Work the case file first, read the setup, then ask yourself: was this one mine?

Let’s find out.
