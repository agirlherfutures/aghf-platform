## Where the words come from
From Section 6, Lesson 18 ("Support & Resistance"): support as an area where price has previously shown buying interest or difficulty continuing lower, resistance as the mirror, "area of interest, not a guaranteed bounce," support ≠ price must go up, resistance ≠ price must go down, and "A level is information. Not permission to enter."
