## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 12, Lesson 3 ("Correction"): correction = price moves against the current directional sequence while the relevant supporting structure stays intact; reversal or invalidation = price changes the information that supported the directional story, read from structure, not one universal candle rule; compare A (the higher low held: correction) with B (closed below the higher low and kept building lower: the story changed); red candles alone decide nothing; "A correction is price moving back. A reversal is price changing the story."
