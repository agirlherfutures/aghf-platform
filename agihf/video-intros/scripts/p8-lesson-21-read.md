# Monthly Review
*Propose. Test. Then decide.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Twenty-One: Monthly Review.

Propose. Test. Then decide.

## 2

Your monthly review zooms out across the whole month.

You spot a new rule you love. Let’s make it official!

Objection! A rule change starts as a hypothesis.

So you write it down: if I change this, what should improve?

Then it goes to a separate study, not straight into your live rules.

Propose. Test. Then decide.

## 3

Inside the study, think of a bakery.

The house recipe is your control. One change is your test.

Same kitchen, same conditions, batch after batch.

The board keeps score, with N under each column.

No swapping the recipe halfway because one batch tasted great.

Only when the batches are in do you review, and decide.

Keep it, drop it, or test longer.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

Test the change before you marry the change.

A hypothesis first. A study second. A decision last.

## 5

Your mission.

Before any rule changes, ask yourself: is this change supported by evidence?

Let’s find out.
