# The Missed Trade
*Missing it isn’t a reason to rewrite it.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Seven: The Missed Trade.

The move happened. You weren’t there. Now what?

## 2

Two neighbours, one comet.

Next door chose to skip it tonight: not her night. That’s a pass, a decision.

Our stargazer planned it all: telescope set, alert set for the exact moment.

Then she popped inside, phone on silent in the other room.

The comet came right on time, and went.

That’s a miss: a valid planned trade that didn’t get executed.

The move happened without her.

## 3

Back at the desk, a thought arrives: next time, I’ll enter earlier.

Freeze. That’s hindsight talking.

With hindsight goggles on, every move looks obvious.

But at the time, entering earlier meant entering without a confirmed continuation.

So what actually failed? Not the method. It produced exactly the trade it should.

The gap was access. Fix the alert, be at the desk, and leave the rules alone.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

A pass is a decision. A miss is an execution gap. And missing it isn’t a reason to rewrite it.

## 5

Your mission.

Next time you miss one, before you change anything, ask yourself: what actually failed?

Let’s find out.
