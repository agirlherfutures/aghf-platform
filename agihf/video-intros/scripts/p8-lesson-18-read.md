# Rule Violation Tracking
*Name it to train it.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Eighteen: Rule Violation Tracking.

Here’s the rule of this lesson: name it to train it.

## 2

Meet the warden. Her ticket book never says bad driver.

It names the exact thing: early entry.

This car feels FOMO, like a big storm cloud.

But it waits for green, and parks.

Trigger noted. No ticket.

This one feels FOMO too, and chases after the window closed.

Two slips: trigger, FOMO. Violation, chased.

What you felt, and what you did, logged separately.

## 3

Now a garage. The owner sighs: this car is just bad.

That’s a label, not a fix. Like telling yourself, you lack discipline.

The mechanic plugs in the diagnostic robot. It counts named codes.

Six of the last twenty setups entered before confirmed continuation.

Now there’s one clear thing to practice: waiting for continuation.

It counts behaviors. It doesn’t label you.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

A pattern you can name is a pattern you can train.

A feeling is a trigger. The behavior is the violation.

## 5

Your mission.

Tag your violations and triggers, then ask yourself: which pattern am I training?

Let’s find out.
