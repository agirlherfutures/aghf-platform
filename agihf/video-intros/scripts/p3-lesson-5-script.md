## Where the words come from
From Section 7, Lesson 5 ("Equal Highs & Equal Lows"): equal highs/lows are highs or lows at roughly the same price, potential buy-side above equal highs and potential sell-side below equal lows, an obvious area worth monitoring, and "A magnet ≠ a guaranteed destination."
