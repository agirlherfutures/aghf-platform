# How to Break Down a Trade

## 1

Welcome to Lesson One: How to Break Down a Trade.

One case. Seven stages. The ending goes last.

## 2

Picture every breakdown as an archaeology dig.

Seven layers. The ending is buried at the very bottom.

The mole wants to tunnel straight to it: did I win or lose?

Not yet. You dig in order: 4H room, 1H map, 15M observe, 1M execute.

Then risk. Then management.

Only then does the outcome chest open.

You judge the dig by the layers, not by the treasure.

## 3

Now picture a film editor cutting the case together.

The cat grabs the last reel and starts the review with: I lost.

Wrong part of the trade. That reel stays taped shut.

Play the reels in order, and pause at the decision.

What did I know here? The thesis, the PIL, the full sequence, the retest, the risk.

Then cut the tape. Stop hit. Process: still valid.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

If your review starts with I lost, you started at the wrong part of the trade.
