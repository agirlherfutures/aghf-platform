## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 13, Lesson 12 ("The Pre-Indication Level"), the PIL (Pre-Indication Level): the structurally relevant level price must prove itself through to begin the sequence; structure creates it, not a line drawn because it looked nice; not every candle high or low, not an FVG edge, random S/R or supply zone (context), not the nearest line, not "whatever makes the setup look prettier"; "We don’t decorate charts"; "If you don’t know your PIL, you’re not ready to label indication."
