# Indicator vs No Indicator
*Your eyes first. Then check your work.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Nine: Indicator vs No Indicator.

Your eyes first. Then check your work.

## 2

Picture a hiker at a fork, map and compass in hand.

GPS stays off. She reads the land with her own eyes first.

She picks the big ridge, the swing price pulled back from, and locks her choice.

Then she switches the GPS on. It points at a little hill: an internal swing.

A difference isn’t automatically her mistake. She reasons it through.

The ridge is on her map. She decides.

## 3

Now picture a dance class with a big mirror.

One student only copies the mirror. The other learns the steps.

Then the teacher draws the curtain.

The copier freezes. The learner keeps dancing, because she knows why each step comes next.

Your indicator is that mirror. Great for checking your form after.

It should make your process faster. Not replace your eyes.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

Your eyes first. Then check your work. The indicator is an assistant. You are the decision-maker.

## 5

Your mission.

Mark this chart with the indicator off, then ask yourself: can I explain the read without it?

Let’s find out.
