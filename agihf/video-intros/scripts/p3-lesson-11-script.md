## Where the words come from
From Phase 3, Section 8, Lesson 11 ("Displacement"): small overlapping candles then a strong, fast, one-directional move with little overlap, what displacement tells you and what it doesn’t (not continuation, not a structure change, not an entry), the "it displaced, I’m buying" mistake, and "momentum is information, not a complete trade plan."
