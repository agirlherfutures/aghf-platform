# What Counts as One Backtest?
*One rep. One evaluated opportunity.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Thirteen: What Counts as One Backtest?

One rep. One evaluated opportunity.

## 2

Here’s a hamster doing replay. The candle counter flies.

But candles aren’t decisions. Stepping through them isn’t a rep.

A setup forms. She evaluates it and takes it. That’s one rep.

Next setup, her rules say no. She passes and logs it. Also one rep.

And a setup she imagines might have formed? Not data. Not a rep.

One rep is one independently evaluated opportunity.

## 3

Now screenshots. Here’s one rep at the bowling alley.

One opportunity, one decision, logged in frame one.

Snap a before shot, an entry shot, an after shot, even a fourth.

Still one rep. The screenshots belong to the rep.

And re-running a session you’ve already seen? You know the ending.

That’s review, not a clean rep.

So be clear about what you’re repeating.

## 4

Remember this.

A rep only helps if you know exactly what you’re repeating.

## 5

Your mission.

Before you log your next rep, ask yourself: what exactly am I repeating?

Let’s find out.
