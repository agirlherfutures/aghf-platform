## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 13, Lesson 15 ("Correction"), correction = a close BACK through the PIL (bullish: indication closed above, correction closes back below; bearish: the mirror); correction is not failure, it’s part of the model; a wick back doesn’t count; "If price never corrects, we don’t invent the correction because we want the trade."
