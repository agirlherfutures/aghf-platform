## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 14, Lesson 27 ("The 15M Checkpoint"), the 15M is the bridge between the higher-timeframe story and execution; recommended for extra confluence, not a requirement; keep your 4H and 1H levels visible ("Same story, closer look."); as price nears your level: directional movement, correcting, or structure beginning to change?; a bullish break of structure (BOS) breaks a relevant previous swing high, a bearish BOS breaks a relevant previous swing low, and only a candle body that closes beyond the level counts ("A wick can visit."); the 15M can begin changing direction before the 1H confirms, which is useful information but does not override the 1H or 4H; move to the 1M only when price is approaching or reacting to a level you marked, without forcing an entry; "The 15M doesn’t give you the entry. It shows you what price is doing on its way to your level."
