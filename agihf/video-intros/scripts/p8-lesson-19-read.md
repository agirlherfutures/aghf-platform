## 3

Quick quiz. Sixty percent win rate. Is it profitable?

Watch two tanks run ten trades each.

Tank one wins often, but small: half an R. Each loss costs a full R.

Tank two wins less, but each win pays two R.

Sixty percent drains. Forty percent fills.

That’s expectancy: what the average trade produces in R, before trading costs.

Win rate alone can’t tell you.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

Win rate alone cannot define profitability.

So start with the part you control: your process.
