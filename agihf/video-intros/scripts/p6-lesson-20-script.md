## Where the words come from
From Phase 6, Section 17, Lesson 20 ("Understanding R"): 1R = what you planned to risk (planned risk $100: −1R = −$100, +1R = +$100, +2R = +$200); Trader A risk $50 result $100 and Trader B risk $250 result $500 are both +2R; risk $150 profit $300 = +2R; +2R doesn’t tell you the trade was valid, well executed, or that the strategy has an edge; "R lets you compare trades without getting hypnotized by dollars."; Dayli: "R doesn’t grade the trade. It translates the outcome into the size of the risk you took."
