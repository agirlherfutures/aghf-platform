## Where the words come from
From Section 2, Lesson 14 ("Retracement vs. Reversal"): Scenario A (the supporting swing holds: possible retracement), Scenario B (the supporting swing breaks and a lower high and lower low form: potential reversal / structural transition), levels not candle color, and the direction of the current move is not always the direction of the larger structure.
