# The AGHF Trade Journal
*Fast. Not “Dear journal…” 😂*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Sixteen: The AGHF Trade Journal.

Fast. Not “Dear journal…”

## 2

Two ways to journal. Option one: Dear journal, today I felt…

Three pages later, the cat’s buried and the stop still isn’t logged.

Option two: the AGHF journal. Five parts, a few minutes.

Trade, setup, execution, mindset, review.

Fast and structured, so you can actually find things later.

And it’s not just trades: passes and missed opportunities belong too.

## 3

Here’s the best part. Finish a rep in replay…

and it lands in your journal by itself.

Instrument, direction, entry, stop, target, screenshots: already filled in.

You review. You don’t retype.

Never type the same trade twice.

Academy cases, backtests, replay, paper, live, manual: every source in one journal.

If the data exists, it’s reused.

The typewriter can keep napping.

## 4

Remember this.

The breakdown is the journal entry. Fast, structured, and never typed twice.

## 5

Your mission.

Log one rep, then ask yourself: what does a useful journal entry look like?

Let’s find out.
