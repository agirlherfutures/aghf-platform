# Weekly Review
*One focus. Not seven.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Twenty: Weekly Review.

One focus. Not seven.

## 2

Your week review shows weeds in seven garden beds.

So you sprint between all of them, pulling one weed here, one weed there.

And nothing actually changes.

Instead, pick one bed for this week.

Weed it properly, root by root.

And that one bed actually blooms.

Many weaknesses. One primary focus. One secondary at most.

## 3

A piano teacher works the same way.

Four bars have pencil marks. She circles just one.

That’s your primary focus for the week.

Then a practice prescription: loop that bar, rep after rep.

And a success measure, so you know when it’s working.

Eight of ten clean. Measured, not guessed.

The focus is what launches your practice.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

Review to find the next adjustment. Not to attack yourself for the entire week.

## 5

Your mission.

Run your weekly review, pick one focus, and ask: what am I training next week?

Let’s find out.
