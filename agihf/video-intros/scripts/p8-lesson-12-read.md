# TradingView Replay
*Uncertainty is what you’re practicing.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Twelve: TradingView Replay.

Uncertainty is what you’re practicing.

## 2

Replay is like karaoke where the screen only shows the line you’re on.

In TradingView, click Replay, pick a date, and start before the session.

From the cut point on, the future is hidden.

Read the 4H, build the 1H map, observe the 15M, then drop to the 1M.

Then press Forward, one candle at a time. No skipping verses.

You don’t know the next line. That’s the point.

## 3

Now practice it in AGHF Replay, where the future is locked.

At every candle, you choose: take, wait, or pass.

Slow is fine. Rushing ahead isn’t.

And no peeking to see what happens.

If you take it, set the stop and target before you advance.

Then record the rep in your journal.

Feels a little uncomfortable? Good. That’s the skill.

## 4

Remember this.

Replay should feel a little uncomfortable. Never jump ahead to see what happens.

## 5

Your mission.

In your first replay session, ask yourself: can I decide without the next candle?

Let’s find out.
