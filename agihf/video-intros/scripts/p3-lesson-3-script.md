## Where the words come from
From Section 7, Lesson 3 ("Why Stops Cluster"): shorts commonly place stops above meaningful highs and longs below meaningful lows, many traders reading the same structure can create clusters of orders, and the chart shows what price did but can’t prove anyone targeted you ("Not a conspiracy").
