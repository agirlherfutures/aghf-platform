## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 13, Lesson 22 ("Clean vs Messy Dayli ICC"), five official classifications: valid + clean, valid but messy, incomplete, invalid, no trade; cleanliness is not a fake score (no "82% clean", no "A+"); messy can be technically valid; clean = one obvious PIL, clear closes in order, an identifiable retest; forced = multiple possible PILs, overlap, rapid close-backs, having to argue; "A valid model should not require a courtroom argument."
