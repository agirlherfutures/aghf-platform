## Where the words come from
From Strategy 2, Module 2, Lesson 4 ("BOS #3: Activating the Zone"): Correction #2 creates a potential zone; an opposite-colour candle appearing is not activation; BOS #3 is a 5M candle close through the point Correction #2 pulled back from (above the high for demand, below the low for supply) and activates the zone; a zone that never gets BOS #3 never activates, so there is no trade; BOS #3 isn’t an entry either: after it, you wait for price to return; "A zone without BOS #3 is just a box on a chart." Zone boundaries (a working rule) are drawn without prices and not stated.
