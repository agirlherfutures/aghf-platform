## Where the words come from
From Phase 4, Section 11, Lesson 15 ("Range Equilibrium"): equilibrium = (high + low) ÷ 2 measured on the selected range (example 20,200 and 20,000 → 20,100), upper half premium and lower half discount (next lesson), and the myth interrupt: price can pass straight through, react, or chop around equilibrium; "a reference, not a magic line"; 50% tells you where you are, not what price will do.
