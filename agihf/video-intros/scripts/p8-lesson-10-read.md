## 1

## 2

Lesson Ten is the full show, and you’re the one reading it.

Like a magic act where the trick is only revealed after the audience guesses.

You go in order: 4H, 1H, 15M, 1M, then risk, then management.

Then, and only then, the cabinet opens.

Read, decide, lock. Then reveal.

## 3

Then comes the review. Picture a sunny window and two sheets of paper.

Your sheet goes up first: the structure, the level, your plan.

Then Dayli’s tracing paper slides on top.

Here your read matched. Here it differed. And here’s one you missed.

It’s not right and wrong. It’s where you matched, where you differed, and why the difference matters.

Can you explain every decision?

## 4

You go first. Dayli reviews after. Explain what you knew, why you acted, and which rule applied.
