## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 13, Lesson 18 ("The Retest"), after I · C · C, the PIL becomes your entry location: price runs away, you do nothing, then wait for the first retest (price returning to the PIL); the first retest is the clean opportunity; repeated returns mean reassess, not automatically the same fresh setup; "Wait for price to come back to you. No chasing while you wait."
