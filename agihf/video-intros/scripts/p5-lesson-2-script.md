## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 12, Lesson 2 ("Indication"): indication = meaningful directional information; it is not a green or red candle (colour is not proof), not a big candle (size is not proof), not an FVG, a sweep or a level touch (events are not proof); it is new information: price progressed beyond a meaningful structural reference; bullish = contained beneath a meaningful high, then progression beyond it; bearish = holding above a meaningful low, then progression below it; "What did price actually prove?"
