# What Backtesting Actually Is
*Finding winners on a finished chart isn’t a test.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Eleven: What Backtesting Actually Is.

Finding winners on a finished chart isn’t a test.

## 2

Meet two pilots practicing landings.

The first one rewinds old landing videos and stars the smooth ones.

Fun, but she already knew how each one ended.

The second pilot sits in a simulator, with the runway in fog.

Each moment, she decides with only what’s in front of her.

That’s a backtest: your rules, applied to history, without the future.

## 3

A real test starts in the lab notebook.

Instrument, session, risk model, stop rule. Then lock it.

Now every rep is tested against exactly those rules.

Twenty reps in, a few losses, and the urge to tweak shows up.

But the rules are locked for this study.

Change a rule, and you’ve started a new version, counting from zero.

Test the rules. Don’t hunt for winners.

## 4

Remember this.

Don’t change the test because you don’t like the results so far.

## 5

Your mission.

Every time you open a chart to practice, ask: am I testing my rules, or finding winners?

Let’s find out.
