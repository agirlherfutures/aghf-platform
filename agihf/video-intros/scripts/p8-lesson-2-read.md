# A Valid Winning Trade
*It won. That’s not why it was good.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Two: A Valid Winning Trade.

It won. That’s not why it was good.

## 2

Picture a cooking show. The dish comes out with a shiny gold tag: plus two R.

The parrot loses it: it won, it won!

But the judge slides the price tag aside and picks up the recipe card.

Thesis agreed. The full sequence closed. Retest entry. Risk set. Plan made first.

That’s what she grades. It was good before it ever won.

## 3

Now a paper plane contest.

She folds it exactly by the plan, crease by crease.

Then she throws, and it lands right on the target.

The dog says: it was good because it flew far.

Nope. Rewind to before the throw.

Thesis, sequence, retest, risk, plan. All true before it left her hand.

A winner is useful when you can say why it deserved to be taken.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

A winner is useful when you can explain why it deserved to be taken before it won.

## 5

Your mission.

Work the case top-down, then answer without the result: why did this trade deserve to be taken?

Let’s find out.
