# Screenshot Journaling
*Before. Entry. After.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Fifteen: Screenshot Journaling.

Before. Entry. After.

## 2

Every rep gets its own photo booth strip.

Three pictures, every time.

Before: the 4H and 1H context at the decision.

Entry: the 1M execution.

After: the outcome, and the structure it left.

Replay shots are labeled for you: date, instrument, timeframe, rep.

Shots from TradingView or live trading? Upload them and attach them to the rep.

The rep keeps its pictures.

## 3

Now open your screenshot library.

Tag each shot. More than one tag is fine.

Clean loser. Chased. A+ retest.

Then filter: show me all my clean losers.

Losses where you followed the rules, lined up in one row.

Now you’re not remembering. You’re seeing patterns.

Try another filter: every chased entry.

The chart keeps the receipts.

## 4

Remember this.

The chart keeps the receipts. Screenshots attach to reps, and tags help you find your patterns.

## 5

Your mission.

Tag a few shots, run a filter, and ask: what does my chart history actually show?

Let’s find out.
