# An Invalid Winning Trade
*It paid. It was still wrong.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Four: An Invalid Winning Trade.

It paid. It was still wrong.

## 2

A frozen pond, and a ranger with three ice lights.

Indication, lit. Correction, lit. Continuation hasn’t closed. The model says wait.

But it looks like it wants to go, so she skates across anyway.

She makes it, and the prize pays out.

Still wrong. Direction right doesn’t make the entry valid.

And the penguin learns the wrong lesson: early works, me next.

That’s the danger of a paid violation.

## 3

Now a museum curator files the case.

The win goes in one case. The violation goes in another.

Stored separately. Never blended.

Then the peacock shows up with a confetti cannon.

No. Never celebrate a violation.

The mouse sweeps up the confetti, and the label stays: warning.

Profitable rule-breaking isn’t evidence. It’s a warning.

This win is trying to teach you the wrong lesson.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

A winner can still be a warning. Never celebrate a violation.

## 5

Your mission.

Spot the entry that broke the model, watch it win anyway, and ask: can a winner be a warning?

Let’s find out.
