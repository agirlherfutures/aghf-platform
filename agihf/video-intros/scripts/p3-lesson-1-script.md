## Where the words come from
From Section 7, Lesson 1 ("What Is Liquidity?"): markets need counterparties (every trade needs two sides), an obvious previous high may attract activity (take-profits, breakout buyers, short stops, new sellers, other orders), we can identify the area but not every motive, and "Liquidity isn’t a secret trap. It’s where orders may be concentrated."
