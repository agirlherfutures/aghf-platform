## Where the words come from
From Phase 5, Section 13, Lesson 16 ("Continuation"), continuation = the close back in the original direction (bullish: close above → close below → close back ABOVE; bearish: the mirror); while price stays on the wrong side, continuation is not confirmed; wicks don’t complete anything; anticipating it is guessing; "You know what you’re waiting for. That doesn’t mean price has done it yet."
