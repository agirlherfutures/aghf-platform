## 1

Welcome to Lesson Seventeen: Setup Quality vs Outcome.

Grade the setup. Record the outcome. Separately.

## 2

Picture a pottery studio where every pot gets two tags.

The grade tag: this vase is a valid A setup.

Today it didn’t sell. The outcome tag says LOSS.

Downgrade it? No. It stays A plus LOSS.

Now a lumpy bowl: a C setup, with a rule violation.

It sells. Plus two R, a win.

Upgrade it? Nope. C, violation, win.

Three facts, stored separately, every time.

## 3

Now, sample size. An astronomer logs two A setups. Both win.

Her parrot squawks: one hundred percent win rate!

Technically true. Educationally terrible. Two out of two is an early sample.

So she keeps watching, night after night, wins and losses alike.

At thirty nights, the picture finally means something.

Every number travels with its sample size: its N.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

Data without sample context can create false confidence.

Quality, validity and outcome are three different things.

## 5

Your mission.

Build your matrix from your own data, and ask: what does my evidence say about quality?

Let’s find out.
