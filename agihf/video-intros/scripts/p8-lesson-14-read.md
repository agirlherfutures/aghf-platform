# Building a Real Sample
*A streak isn’t a sample.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Fourteen: Building a Real Sample.

Because a streak isn’t a sample.

## 2

Meet the coin-flip machine. It’s perfectly fair.

Tails. Tails. Tails.

Three in a row, and the goat panics: it’s broken, change the rules!

Three flips can’t tell you anything. Keep flipping.

After a hundred, the picture looks completely different.

Three reps: learn from them. Don’t conclude from them.

So three losses in a row don’t mean the plan is broken.

## 3

Your evidence meter works like a beehive.

Three cells in, a bear tastes three jars and calls the whole hive bad.

Three jars? Learn from them. Don’t conclude from them.

Your numbers always show N, so you know how early it is.

Ten, twenty-five, fifty, a hundred: practice milestones.

Nice to reach. Not proof of an edge.

Keep filling the hive.

## 4

Remember this.

Don’t make a permanent rule from a temporary streak.

## 5

Your mission.

Next time a streak tempts you, ask: is this enough to conclude anything?

Let’s find out.
