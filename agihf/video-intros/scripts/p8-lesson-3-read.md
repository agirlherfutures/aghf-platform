# A Valid Losing Trade
*The stop got hit. The trade didn’t change.*

Read these lines exactly as written. Coaching notes from the script are left out.

## 1

Welcome to Lesson Three: A Valid Losing Trade.

The stop got hit. The trade didn’t change.

## 2

Picture a gardener who plants by the book.

Thesis, sequence, retest, risk. Every check passes.

The sprout comes up. Then, out of nowhere, hail.

The stop gets hit. Minus one R.

The snail panics: change everything!

Based on what? The planting was right. The hail came after.

So she plants the next row exactly the same way.

One storm is not a sample.

## 3

Now, a lab. Every trade is one marble in a sample jar.

Today’s marble is a loss, and the raccoon goes straight for the rewrite button.

The scientist covers it. Not on one valid loss.

The loss was the planned loss. It goes in the jar with everything else.

Graded on process, same as a valid win.

The stop getting hit didn’t rewrite the trade.

## 4

Here’s the big takeaway.

The stop getting hit does not rewrite the trade that existed before the stop got hit.

## 5

Your mission.

Work a trade that passes every check, watch it lose, and ask: does one loss rewrite a valid trade?

Let’s find out.
