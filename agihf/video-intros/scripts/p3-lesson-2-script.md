## Where the words come from
From Section 7, Lesson 2 ("Buy-Side & Sell-Side"): above highs is buy-side liquidity (short stops, breakout buys), below lows is sell-side liquidity (long stops, breakdown sells), and it describes where orders may rest, not where price must go ("Not a destination").
