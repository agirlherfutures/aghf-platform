Trend or Range?

A chart loads. You call it — is price trending or ranging? This is how you find the no-trade zones before you ever waste a dollar.

📊
Know before you trade.

Most new traders lose money in ranges because they try to force trades that aren't there. The first skill is knowing when to trade and when to sit on your hands.

A chart loads with recent price action
Trending = higher highs & higher lows (up) OR lower lows & lower highs (down)
Range = price bouncing between two levels — no real direction
Ranging markets = no trade zone. Wait for the breakout.
