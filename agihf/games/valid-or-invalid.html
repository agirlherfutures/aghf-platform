The most important skill in ICC isn't finding setups — it's filtering them. A wick is not an indication. A bounce is not a correction. This game trains your filter.

Judge it: is this a valid ICC trade or not?
Valid = a close past the PIL (Indication), a close back past it (Correction), a close back in the original direction (Continuation), then the first retest
Invalid = a wick instead of a close, a missing step, or a chased entry
When in doubt — it's probably invalid. ICC has strict rules.
