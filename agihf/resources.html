Quick Answers

Trader FAQ

Quick answers to some of the questions new traders ask most while getting started.

What market does Dayli trade?

Futures — using the Dayli ICC method on the 1-minute timeframe, along with supply and demand on the 5-minute timeframe.

What platform do I need?

TradingView is what Dayli uses and teaches from. You'll also want a broker or prop firm account to actually place trades.

What is TradingView?

A charting platform for watching and analyzing markets in real time. It's free to start, and it's where most AGHF lessons are taught from.

Do I need a prop firm?

Not to start learning. Prop firms let you trade their capital after passing an evaluation — a common next step once you're ready to trade live without risking your own full account.

What is a futures evaluation?

A test phase where a prop firm checks that you can trade profitably and follow their rules before funding your account.

When does the market open?

Futures trade nearly 24 hours a day, Sunday evening through Friday afternoon, with a short daily maintenance break. AGHF's lessons focus on the most active sessions.

What are micros and minis?

Micros (MNQ, MGC) are smaller-sized contracts built for smaller accounts. Minis (NQ, GC) are the full-sized version of the same markets.

Where should I start inside AGHF?

Start with University Basics — it's built in order for a reason. Come back to this Resource Library any time you need a quick reference.
